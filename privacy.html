Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Overview. This is a static personal portfolio. It has no account system, advertising scripts, analytics platform, database or server-side contact form.

Contact form. The form does not send submitted values to this website. Your browser opens your configured email application with a pre-filled message. Your email provider then handles the message under its own policies.

Local storage. The site stores only the light/dark theme preference in browser localStorage. It is not sent to the portfolio owner.

Third parties. Typography is loaded from Google Fonts. The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. External links may send normal request metadata to the destination service. No third-party tracker or social widget is embedded.

Public information. The professional email address shown on this site is intentionally public. Please do not submit passwords, financial information, government IDs or other sensitive information through the contact form.

Changes. Material changes to data practices will be reflected here with an updated date.
